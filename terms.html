Text message terms

These terms cover text messages (SMS) from MJL Collective LLC (“MJL Collective,” “we,” or “us”). By turning on text alerts in the MJL Collective client portal, you agree to these terms.

1. Program name

MJL Collective client portal alerts

2. What the program is

MJL Collective builds and maintains websites for its clients. Clients send change requests and questions through the client portal at https://www.mjlcollective.com/clients. When we reply to a request or post an update on your project, we can send you a short text with the start of our reply and a link back to the portal.

These texts are about your projects only. We do not send marketing or promotional texts through this program.

3. How you sign up

Text alerts are off by default. To turn them on, sign in to the client portal, open the Alerts page, check “Text me when MJL replies,” enter your mobile number, and save. You can turn them off on the same page at any time. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

4. How often

Message frequency varies. You get at most one text each time we reply to one of your requests, so how many you get depends on how often we reply.

5. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. We do not charge for texts, but your mobile carrier’s normal rates and plan terms apply.

6. How to stop

Reply STOP to any text to stop getting texts from us. We will send one text to confirm that you are unsubscribed, and then no more. You can also turn text alerts off on the Alerts page. To start again, reply START or turn text alerts back on.

7. How to get help

Reply HELP to any text for help, or email mitchell@mjlcollective.com.

8. Carriers

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Texts are delivered on a best-effort basis and may be delayed or not delivered for reasons outside our control. Supported carriers may change.

9. Your privacy

We use your mobile number only to send portal project alerts. We never sell it or share it with third parties or affiliates for marketing. See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information.

10. Changes and contact

We may update these terms. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the “Last updated” date.
