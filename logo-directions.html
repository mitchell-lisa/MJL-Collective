Four directions for the MJL Collective mark.

Every mark is drawn by hand as SVG and set in one color. Where type appears it is Instrument Sans, the face the site already uses, and it will be converted to outlines in the final files. Each direction is shown at the four sizes the mark has to live at: the header at 82 pixels, the favicon at 32, the email signature at 132, and large at 480. Once on white, once on ink.

A. StairMonoline letters, the L climbs

Three letters built from straight strokes of one weight, with the foot of the L stepping up into the three treads of Build, Maintain, Grow. It comes from the mark you have now and the stone stairway you photographed in Italy, redrawn so the stair reads at 32 pixels and the letters stand square instead of leaning.

B. LigatureThe M carries the J

The last leg of the M runs below the line and hooks back, and that hook is the J, so the three letters become one built object with the L standing beside it. It is the most compact of the four and the one that embroiders and stamps best, because there is nothing small in it. The name sits to the right in sentence case, the way an architecture practice sets its own.

C. HallmarkA block with the name struck out of it

A solid square, the proportions of one glass block from your photograph, with the letters and the word cut out of it rather than printed on it. It behaves like a maker's mark: a stamp on an invoice, a woven label, a patch on a hat, a plate by a door, and it needs no second color because the ground shows through the letters.

D. MastheadA pure wordmark, no symbol

No symbol at all: the three letters set large and tight in Instrument Sans, the word set small and spaced to exactly the same width beneath, held between two rules like the nameplate on a newspaper. It is the quietest of the four and the closest to the Aimé Leon Dore reference, and it carries the whole brand on typography, which is also what the site does.
