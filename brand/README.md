# MJL Collective brand

## The mark

Three letters built from one stroke weight. The M's last leg runs past the
baseline and hooks left, and that hook is the J, so the monogram is a single
connected shape. The L stands beside it on the same baseline. "Collective" sits
to the right in sentence case.

Two lockups:

- **Lockup** (`mjl-collective.svg`): the monogram and the word together. This is
  the default and what the site header, the footer and the emails use.
- **Mark** (`mjl-collective-mark.svg`): the monogram alone. Use it only where
  the lockup cannot fit or the name is already present: the favicon, an app
  icon, a stamp, a patch.

The type in every file is converted to outlines, so nothing depends on a font
being installed. The source geometry lives in `assets/site.css` sizing and in
the generator that produced these files.

| --- | --- |

| `mjl-collective-mark-white.svg` | Monogram alone, white. |
| `mjl-collective-on-white-512.png` / `-2048.png` | Lockup on white. |
| `mjl-collective-on-ink-512.png` / `-2048.png` | Lockup on ink. |
| `mjl-collective-mark-on-white-512.png` | Monogram on white. |
| `mjl-collective-mark-on-ink-512.png` | Monogram on ink. |

Prefer the SVG everywhere it is accepted. Use a PNG only where a vector is not
allowed, such as an embroidery brief or an email signature.

## Palette

Sampled from the glass block photograph on the site.

| Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#1b1c1b` | The mark, headings, body text |
| Poured | `#2a2c2a` | Dark grounds: the top bar, the footer, the favicon |
| Cement | `#eceeec` | The page ground, panels, the social card |
| Plaster | `#f5f6f5` | The page background |
| Mortar | `#cfd3d0` | Hairlines and borders |
| Ink 2 | `#4e504e` | Secondary text |
| Ink 3 | `#888b88` | Labels and captions |

There is no accent color. The mark works in one color and should stay that way.
On any other ground, use ink or white, whichever holds more contrast.

## Typeface

Instrument Sans. The wordmark is set at weight 500 with slightly negative
tracking, then converted to outlines. The site pairs it with Archivo for the
Build, Maintain and Grow headings only. Two faces is the ceiling.

## Minimum sizes

| Asset | Screen | Print |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lockup | 110 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Monogram alone | 20 px wide | 6 mm wide |

Below the lockup minimum the word stops resolving. Use the monogram instead of
shrinking the lockup further.

## Clear space

Keep a margin on all four sides equal to half the height of the M, measured
from the outer edge of the artwork. Nothing else sits inside it: no text, no
rule, no edge of a photograph. More space is always allowed.

## What not to do

Do not add a gradient, a glow, an outline or a drop shadow. Do not re-set the
word in another typeface. Do not stretch, condense or rotate the artwork. Do
not change the spacing between the monogram and the word. Do not place the mark
on a busy photograph without a solid panel behind it. Do not recolor it.
