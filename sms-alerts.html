Text alerts: how clients opt in

Lets Link LLC, doing business as MJL Collective client portal project alerts
Last updated: October 12, 2026

Who gets these texts

MJL Collective is a web design and development studio. Our website clients use the MJL client portal at mjlcollective.com/clients to send change requests for their sites. Clients can choose to get a text when MJL Collective replies to a request or posts an update on their project. These are transactional project alerts only, never marketing. Text alerts are off by default.

How to opt in

  1. Sign in to the MJL client portal at mjlcollective.com/clients.
  2. Open the Alerts page (mjlcollective.com/clients/alerts).
  3. Check “Text me when MJL replies” and enter your own mobile number. The box is never checked for you.
  4. Click Save. You can turn text alerts off on the same page at any time, or reply STOP.

Mobile numbers are collected only this way. We do not buy lists or add numbers for clients.

The consent wording shown next to the checkbox

By turning on text alerts, you agree to receive texts from MJL Collective about updates and replies on your projects at this number. At most one text per reply. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of any purchase. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

Screenshots of the Alerts page

Step 1, default state. This is what every client sees when they open the Alerts page: the “Text me when MJL replies” box is unchecked, the mobile number field is empty, and the email box is unchecked. Text alerts stay off until the client checks the box themselves (sample client, test data only).

Step 2, after the client opts in. The client checks the box themselves, types their own mobile number, and clicks Save.

Sample text

MJL Collective replied to your request: Your new homepage hours are live. Take a look and tell me if anything should change. Open https://www.mjlcollective.com/clients Reply STOP to opt out

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to any text to stop. Reply HELP for help, or email mitchell@mjlcollective.com. Message frequency varies (at most one text per reply). Message and data rates may apply. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Mobile numbers and text opt-in data are never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing.
